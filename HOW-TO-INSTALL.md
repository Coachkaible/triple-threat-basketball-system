# How to Install the Triple Threat App

Players can install the system on their phones so it works like a regular app (including offline).

## On iPhone (Safari)

1. Open the site in Safari:  
   https://triple-threat-basketball-system.coachkaible.workers.dev/
2. Tap the Share button (square with arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Name it “Triple Threat” and tap Add.
5. The app icon will appear on your home screen.

## On Android (Chrome)

1. Open the site in Chrome.
2. Tap the menu (three dots) in the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
4. Confirm. The app icon will appear on your home screen.

## Tips

- After installing, open the app from the home screen icon (not the browser).
- The first time you open it, stay online so it can cache the content.
- After that it works offline.
- If you update the system later, players may need to clear the cache or re-install once to get the new version.
